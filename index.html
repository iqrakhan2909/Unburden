PRIVACY, WITHOUT THE FINE PRINT

Your brain dump is yours.

Unburden uses an open model through Transformers.js in your browser. Your text is passed to that model locally for inference; it is not sent to a hosted AI chat API by this app.

Honest caveat: the first model download needs internet and the model files are fetched from the Hugging Face Hub. After download, browser caching may help, but fully offline use is not guaranteed. Avoid entering highly sensitive information.
